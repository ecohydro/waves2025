# Design decisions
Decision: existing dryland_ecohydrology.jpg, visually inspected (1200 × 279), provides water, vegetation, people and livestock in one authentic panorama. Sensor close-up was too busy; community photograph would place disproportionate emphasis on identifiable people. Do not upscale into a tall full-screen background.
Decision: separate solid-blue text panel and panoramic image; avoids darkening the photograph or relying on variable pixel contrast. Mobile crops centrally at 2:1, desktop near native 4:1. No image editing or generated imagery needed.
